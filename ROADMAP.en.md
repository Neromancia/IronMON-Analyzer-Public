# Future updates

IronMON Analyzer will continue to evolve based on feedback from actual runs. These plans have no guaranteed release dates.

- **Refine move recommendations**: better distinguish between similar plans, explain their trade-offs and fix inconsistencies encountered during runs.
- **Improve readability and the reduced layout**: adjust the interface for desktop use and player feedback.
- **Make profile backups and transfers easier**: help players find their files and simplify restoring a profile on another PC.
- **Maintain the maps**: correct markers, names and annotations reported by the community.
- **Develop Monaris further**: gradually expand the collection and the experience of discovering cards.
- **Explore other games and modes**: choose future additions based on requests and testing capacity, without announcing compatibility before validation.

To suggest an improvement: [contact.ironmonanalyzer@gmail.com](mailto:contact.ironmonanalyzer@gmail.com).
